# SLATE: Instructor Roster

How NIBM School of Computing plans who teaches, who covers non-teaching work, who holds the night, and who is away, and publishes that plan to the people doing it.

## People

**Cadre**:
The teaching staff who can be given Duty and Night Duty: the Demonstrators and Technical Instructors (currently nine, including the Technical Assistant). Shared Terminals, Executives and Admins are not Cadre.
_Avoid_: Instructors (as a collective), staff, team

**Demonstrator**:
A Cadre member who also plans and publishes the roster and reviews Leave. A Demonstrator's own Leave is Notified Leave.
_Avoid_: Roster master, planner

**Executive**:
Campus leadership who watch the roster and review Leave, but are never given Duty and never plan the roster.
_Avoid_: Director, admin, cockpit user

**Shared Terminal**:
A login used by many people at a campus lab or lobby to view the roster; it belongs to no one and is never given Duty.
_Avoid_: Kiosk account, instructors account

## Work

**Duty**:
One Cadre member committed to one Time Block on one date, for one Duty Type (teaching, CGU call handling, lab inspection, and so on).
_Avoid_: Assignment, allocation, slot, session (as the umbrella term)

**Duty Type**:
The kind of work a Duty is; maintained by Demonstrators in the Catalog.
_Avoid_: Task type, category

**Teaching Session**:
A Duty whose Duty Type is teaching: a batch, a module and usually a room.
_Avoid_: Lecture, class, lab session (as distinct concepts)

**Time Block**:
A fixed part of the teaching day: Morning, Afternoon, or the Sunday CCS block. A Duty that fills both Morning and Afternoon for the same class is a Full Day.
_Avoid_: Slot, period, shift

**Night Duty**:
The single Cadre member responsible for the campus overnight on a given date. It is not a Duty: it has no Time Block, and there is exactly one per date.
_Avoid_: Night shift, caretaker, night duty type

## Availability

**Leave**:
A Cadre member's request to be away for one or more whole dates (never part of a day). Once approved, it blocks Duty and Night Duty on those dates.
_Avoid_: Holiday, absence, off day

**Leave status**:
Pending until reviewed; then Approved or Rejected by a Leave Reviewer, or Cancelled when the applicant withdraws it (before or after approval, but never once it has ended).
_Avoid_: Declined, revoked

**Notified Leave**:
Leave taken by a Demonstrator. It is Approved the moment it is submitted; the other Leave Reviewers are told rather than asked.
_Avoid_: Self-approved leave, auto-approval

**Withdrawal**:
The applicant cancelling their own Pending or upcoming Approved Leave; the Leave becomes Cancelled and its dates are free for Duty again.
_Avoid_: Revoke, delete, retract

**Leave Reviewer**:
A Demonstrator, Executive or Admin deciding a Pending Leave. Nobody reviews their own Leave, and a decided Leave is never re-decided.
_Avoid_: Approver, authority

**Free Standby**:
A Cadre member who, in a given Time Block, has no Duty and no approved Leave. It is worked out, never assigned, and Night Duty does not affect it.
_Avoid_: Available, idle, spare

## Planning

**Roster Week**:
The seven-day unit that is planned as a Draft and then Published to the Cadre.
_Avoid_: Week (unqualified), schedule

**Published**:
A Roster Week that has been announced to the Cadre. It stays editable; changes after publishing are corrections, and the affected Cadre members are told.
_Avoid_: Locked, final, frozen

**Draft**:
A Roster Week still being planned and not yet announced.
_Avoid_: Unpublished, pending

**Planning Window**:
Any seven consecutive days a person is viewing or editing; it need not line up with a Roster Week.
_Avoid_: Horizon, week view

**Catalog**:
The shared lists Demonstrators maintain for planning: batches, rooms, modules and Duty Types.
_Avoid_: Presets, settings
